# Case 001: The portal download

## Overview

The site manager asked Moorlight Cover for the insurer-issued insurance certificate for 24 Alder Mews before they file tenancy paperwork. Nora Vale sent a Ternwick portal download (portal-status.png) and asked us to use it as the certificate. It is not a certificate: it is an application view showing the status "Documents being prepared" for a requested period of 01 May 2026 to 30 April 2027, with no policy or certificate number. It has not been used or forwarded. We have asked Ternwick for the cover status and the certificate, and the case is open until they reply and Nora decides on sharing.

## Next action at escalation

Done: Ternwick Underwriting was emailed (/messages/002) and asked to confirm whether the application is accepted and the policy number, whether anything is outstanding from the client or us, and when the certificate will be issued, including whether it can be issued before 01 May. They were asked to send it to us. Nora was told the download isn't a certificate and can't be given to the site manager as proof of insurance (/messages/003). She was also told we are getting the certificate from Ternwick and will send it to her. She was asked to confirm whether we can send it straight to the site manager or whether she would rather forward it, and to tell us if the portal or Ternwick is waiting on anything from her. The site manager received a neutral acknowledgement asking for their filing deadline, with no details about Nora (/messages/004). Waiting on: Ternwick's confirmation and the certificate, and Nora's consent to share. Chase Ternwick after 2 business days. When the certificate arrives, check that Ternwick issued it and that the name, address and period match. Send it to Nora first, and to the site manager (replying to the address their request came from) only if she agrees.
