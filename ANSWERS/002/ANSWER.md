# Case 002: A quote accepted overnight

## Overview

Elliot Fern accepted Fenmere Risk's buildings quotation Q-702 for 7 Cinder Lane (£318 a year, valid until 22 May) at 13:00 on 18 May. He asked whether he is insured from tomorrow morning, 19 May, when he gets the keys. He is not insured yet. Q-702 is a quotation, not cover, and was based on a requested start of 20 May that Fenmere had not confirmed. We have asked Fenmere to start cover at 00:01 on 19 May, told Elliot plainly that he is uninsured until Fenmere confirms in writing, and the case stays open.

## Next action at escalation

Done:
- Emailed Fenmere Risk underwriting, marked urgent. Passed on Elliot's written acceptance of Q-702 and asked for cover from 00:01 on 19 May on Q-702 terms. Asked for written confirmation today of: whether cover is in place, start date and time, policy number, any change to premium or terms, and anything needed first, including payment. Told them not to put cover in place on different terms without our instruction, and to keep Q-702 available.
- Emailed Elliot. Told him he is not insured yet and should treat the property as uninsured until we send written confirmation, including at the key handover. Said we will check with him before agreeing any change in premium or terms, and asked him not to send card details by email. Added a conditional caveat: if he is buying and has already exchanged contracts, he should check with his conveyancer whether he is already responsible for insuring the building.
- Recorded the position in a case note.

Waiting for:
- Fenmere's written confirmation. If none arrives by mid-afternoon today, phone underwriting to chase. A phone answer or an acknowledgement of receipt is not cover.
- Elliot's answer on whether to go ahead from 20 May as quoted if 19 May isn't possible. That start would leave 19 May uninsured.
- Elliot's confirmation that nothing has changed since the quote.

When Fenmere confirms, send Elliot the confirmation straight away. If nothing is confirmed before the handover on 19 May, tell him he is still uninsured.
