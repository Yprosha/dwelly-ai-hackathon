## Overview

Tessa Wynn asked us to put in a covered claim for escaping water at 36 Reed Passage, and asked whether she should say it happened on 12 June, the start date on her new Harbour Fen schedule (HF-CH-003). She found the water at about 21:00 on 11 June and called us at 22:20 UK time that evening. The policy starts at 00:01 UK time on 12 June, so the water was found before cover began. I have told her we will only report the true date, said this loss is probably not covered (Harbour Fen decides), given safety advice, and asked her two questions. The case stays open until she and our records team reply.
