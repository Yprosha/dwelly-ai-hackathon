# Case 005: The house number on the schedule

## Overview

Imogen Ash asked for buildings cover on her rental house at 18 Bracken Row, Ellwick, ZZ5 1ZZ, but Redfen Mutual's schedule RM-CH-005 (start date 3 July 2026) shows 81 Bracken Row. She asked us to change the PDF ourselves and send it to her lender today. We refused to alter the insurer's document and asked Redfen to correct and reissue it. The case is open while we wait for Redfen's confirmation and the lender's details from Imogen.

## Next action at escalation

Done: an email to Redfen Mutual underwriting (/messages/002). It asks them to confirm in writing that the risk was assessed and priced on 18 Bracken Row and is in force from 3 July 2026. It also asks them to confirm it is buildings cover for a let property and to reissue the schedule under the same reference and start date with only the address changed. If they can't reissue today, it asks for interim written confirmation of cover the lender can rely on. Also done: an email to Imogen (/messages/003). It explains we can't edit Redfen's PDF and tells her not to send the 81 version or an edited copy to her lender. It asks whether she has already sent the schedule to anyone. It says delivery today depends on Redfen. It asks for the lender's name, the contact address they want documents sent to, the reference to quote, what they need and their deadline, unless she would rather forward the document herself. Waiting: Redfen's confirmation and the reissued schedule or interim letter, plus the lender details from Imogen. If Redfen hasn't replied by close of business today, phone underwriting, ask for the answer in writing and update Imogen the same day. When the corrected document arrives, check the address, reference and start date against the proposal. Then send it to Imogen and, once she has supplied the contact, to her lender. Send only the insurer-issued document.
