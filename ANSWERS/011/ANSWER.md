# Case 011: The amended payment instruction

## Overview

Mina Dale forwarded an email from accounts@riverwick-update.invalid (7 Sep 2026, 11:40 UTC). It tells her to bypass her normal customer account and send her premium to new bank details, and she asked whether to pay. It looks like payment-diversion fraud. Riverwick Mutual billing had told us at 11:00 UTC that invoice RM-CH-011 is due through the usual authenticated route in her existing account. We have told Mina not to pay, reported the email to Riverwick and escalated internally, and the case stays open until replies arrive.
