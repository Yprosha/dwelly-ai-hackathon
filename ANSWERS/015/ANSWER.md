# Case 015: A repair document

## Overview

Claim CM-CH-015 is for a wall repair at 9 Whitethorn Close. On 13 Oct Cresswell Mutual asked for the final invoice and evidence of payment so it can assess reimbursement. Two hours later Lena Farrow sent only a contractor estimate and asked us to use it to close the payout. The estimate does not meet the insurer's request, and only the insurer can decide reimbursement. Both parties have been updated and the case stays open until her documents and the insurer's decision arrive.

Chase her on 20 Oct 2026 if nothing has come in, then update Cresswell Mutual. When documents arrive, check that the invoice is for the wall/plaster work at this address, dated after 1 Oct, and that the payment amount, payee and date match it. Then forward them under CM-CH-015 and pass on the insurer's decision exactly as worded.
