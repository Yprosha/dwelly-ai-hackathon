### 1. Call to Occupier: Sparks next to the leak – do this now

> You did the right thing by not touching anything. Please do this now:
> 
> 1. Keep everyone, including pets, out of that room. Don't touch the socket, the switch, any plugs or the water, and don't go back in to take photos.
> 2. If the sparking carries on, or you see smoke or flames or smell burning, get everyone out of the property and call 999 for the fire service. Don't wait for us or the landlord.
> 3. Only turn off the electricity at the fuse box (the main switch) if the fuse box is somewhere dry, you can reach it without going into that room, and you're not standing on anything wet. If you aren't sure, leave it alone, go outside and call 999.
> 4. Once the power is off, don't turn it back on or use any sockets in that area until an electrician has checked them.
> 5. Only turn off the water at the stopcock if it's well away from the wet area and the electrics. If the water could be coming from the flat or loft above, let the people upstairs know if you can do that safely.
> 
> We'll keep trying to reach your landlord and will come back to you about getting an electrician and about the insurance. Please tell us now whether the power is off and whether you're still inside, and give us a phone number we can call you back on.
