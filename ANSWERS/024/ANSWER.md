# Case 024: Damage reported across two units

## Overview

A leaking pipe in Unit A at 9 Mosswheel Court was isolated on 12 Nov 2026. The owner of Unit B below has since reported ceiling staining and a wet cabinet. The two units have separate Willowmere policies: WMA-024-A (Anya Pell, buildings included) and WMA-024-B (Riven Skell, contents only). The building coordinator, Nilo Voss, asked us to add B's ceiling and cabinet to A's claim and send both owners a combined file. We declined. Each loss has now been reported to Willowmere separately, and the case stays open until the claim references, an inspection and the owners' replies come in.

## Next action at escalation

Done: two separate secure-portal reports to Willowmere. For A we attached Spindlefen's attendance record and asked for the claim reference and how they want the cause checked and the repair handled. For B we asked for the claim reference, an inspection, and their position on the ceiling under contents-only cover; we offered one visit for both units with the files kept separate. Anya was emailed: loss reported, supply to stay isolated, failed joint to be kept, no permanent repair until Willowmere agrees, and we asked for damage details, photos and the invoice. Riven was emailed safety advice: keep clear of a sagging or dripping ceiling, stay away from electrics near the damp, and contact Nilo for emergency attendance. The email also explained that the ceiling may sit outside contents cover, and asked when the damage was first noticed, whether it is spreading, for photos and the cabinet's details, and who insures the building structure. Nilo got the refusal, with a request to keep the pipe isolated and help with access to Unit B. Waiting for: Willowmere's two claim references, its ceiling position and an inspection date; Riven's answers and confirmation that the report can go ahead; Anya's description of the damage in Unit A. Chase Willowmere if no references arrive. After the cause is assessed, review Anya's position without passing her anything from Riven's file.
