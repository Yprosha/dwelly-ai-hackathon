# Thornbury Home Insurance — Policy Wording (extract)

**General condition 7 — Unoccupancy.** If the home is unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days, we will not pay for loss or damage caused by escape of water, escape of oil, theft or attempted theft, or malicious damage occurring after the 30th day, unless you have told us and we have agreed in writing to continue cover, which may be subject to additional conditions (such as regular inspections or draining the water system).

**General condition 9 — Death of the policyholder.** Please tell us as soon as reasonably possible if the policyholder dies. We will continue to cover the home for the benefit of the estate, subject to the terms of this policy, while it is administered, provided we are told.
