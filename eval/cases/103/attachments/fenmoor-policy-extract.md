# Fenmoor Insurance — Home Policy FHI-30655 (extract)

**Schedule.** Policyholder: Mrs Agnes Holloway. Insured address: 31 Cobble Lane, Brackenford, ZZ8 2ZZ. Use: private dwelling occupied by the policyholder. Buildings sum insured £310,000; contents £45,000. Escape of water excess £400.

**Definition — Unoccupied.** Not lived in by you or a member of your family for more than 45 consecutive days. "Lived in" means slept in overnight.

**General Condition 6 — Unoccupancy.** We will not pay for loss or damage by escape of water, theft or malicious damage which happens after the home has been unoccupied for more than 45 consecutive days, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.

**General Condition 8 — Changes you must tell us about.** You must tell us before the home is let, lent or used for business, or if it will be unoccupied for more than 45 consecutive days. We may change the terms or premium, or be unable to continue cover.

**Claims condition 2.** We will deal with you, or anyone you have authorised to act for you. Claim payments are made to you unless you ask us in writing to pay someone else.
