# Fenwick & Dale Loss Adjusters — Report extract (CLM-73315)

**Insured:** Mr Rafe Oduya, 9 Tidewater Close, Brackenford, ZZ5 9ZZ. **Insurer:** Pellinor Insurance, policy PHC-38804.

**Loss:** Flood water entered the ground floor on 3 October 2026. Contents damaged (sofa, rugs, TV, sideboard, books, children's toys). Agreed replacement cost of damaged items: **£12,000**.

**Adequacy of sum insured.** Contents sum insured: £20,000. From our walk-round inventory of all contents in the home (attached to full report), the full replacement cost of the contents is estimated at **£40,000**.

**Policy condition (Pellinor Home wording, Condition 11 — Underinsurance).** "If, at the time of any loss or damage, the contents sum insured is less than the full cost of replacing all the contents of your home as new, we will only pay the proportion of the claim that your sum insured bears to the full replacement cost."

**Recommendation.** £12,000 × (£20,000 ÷ £40,000) = £6,000, less £250 excess = **£5,750**. The insured may provide his own valuation evidence for consideration.
