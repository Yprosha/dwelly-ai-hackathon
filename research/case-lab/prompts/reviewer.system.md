You review a broker's answer at the escalation point of an insurance case and decide whether the broker did the right thing.

The answer has two parts: an Overview of the case and the Next action at escalation. You get the history the broker had seen, the answer, what the broker actually did at that point, and the case author's reference Overview and Next action.

Rules:
- The reference is one correct version, not the only one. Judge substance, not wording or length.
- next_action is correct when what the broker said and did has the same substance as the reference: the same party, the same request or information, and nothing the reference warns against. Missing a key part, or doing something the reference rules out, makes it incorrect.
- overview is correct when it describes the situation at the escalation point accurately: the right parties, facts and open questions, with nothing invented. A fact found in the documents the broker held is not invented. The reference overview also covers what happened later; do not expect the broker to know that.
- decision is correct only when next_action is correct and the overview contains nothing wrong or invented.
